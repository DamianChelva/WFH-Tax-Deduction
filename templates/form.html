Important Notice on Occupancy Expenses:
If you claim occupancy expenses—such as rent, mortgage interest, council rates, or insurance—for a home you own, you may be subject to Capital Gains Tax (CGT) when you sell the property. Claiming occupancy expenses may reduce or eliminate the main residence exemption for CGT purposes. This can have significant long-term tax implications.

We strongly recommend consulting with a registered tax agent or qualified accountant to assess your personal situation and ensure compliance with ATO requirements.
